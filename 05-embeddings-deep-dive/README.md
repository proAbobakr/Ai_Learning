## Why Learn Embeddings?

Embeddings are the **foundation** of modern AI applications:

- **Search Engines** - Find semantically similar content
- **Recommendation Systems** - "Users who liked X also liked Y"
- **Chatbots** - Understand user intent
- **RAG Systems** - Retrieve relevant documents for LLMs
- **Clustering** - Group similar items together
- **Anomaly Detection** - Find outliers in data
