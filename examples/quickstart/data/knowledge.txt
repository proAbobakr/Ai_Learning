Company Benefits and Policies

Our company offers the following benefits to all full-time employees:

1. Health Insurance
Full medical, dental, and vision coverage is provided to all employees. The company pays 80% of the premium costs, and employees pay the remaining 20%. Coverage begins on the first day of employment. Family members can be added to the plan at an additional cost.

2. Vacation and Time Off
Employees receive 15 days of paid vacation per year, which accrue at a rate of 1.25 days per month. Additionally, employees get 10 sick days per year. Vacation requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance and are subject to manager approval. Unused vacation days can be carried over to the next year, up to a maximum of 5 days.

3. Remote Work Policy
Employees are allowed to work remotely up to 3 days per week. Remote work must be scheduled in advance and requires manager approval. All remote employees must be available during core hours from 10 AM to 3 PM EST. A stable internet connection and proper home office setup are required. VPN access is mandatory when working remotely, and remote employees must attend all scheduled team meetings via video conference.

4. Retirement Benefits
The company offers a 401(k) retirement plan with company matching. We match up to 5% of your salary in 401(k) contributions. Employees are immediately eligible to participate in the 401(k) plan upon hire, and company matching begins after 90 days of employment.

5. Professional Development
We invest in our employees' growth and development. Each employee receives an annual budget of $2,000 for professional development activities, including courses, conferences, certifications, and workshops. Employees must submit a request for approval before registering for any professional development activity.

6. Parental Leave
New parents (both mothers and fathers) are eligible for 12 weeks of paid parental leave. This leave can be taken within the first year after the birth or adoption of a child. Employees must provide at least 30 days notice before taking parental leave, when possible.

7. Employee Wellness Program
We offer a comprehensive wellness program that includes:
- Free gym membership at partnered fitness centers
- Monthly wellness workshops on topics like nutrition, stress management, and mental health
- Annual health screenings
- Reimbursement for wellness activities up to $500 per year

8. Company Holidays
The company observes all federal holidays plus 2 additional floating holidays per year that employees can use at their discretion. The office is closed on Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day.

9. Flexible Working Hours
While core hours are 10 AM to 3 PM EST, employees have flexibility in their start and end times. Some employees choose to start early (7 AM) and leave early, while others prefer to start later and work into the evening. Discuss your preferred schedule with your manager.

10. Employee Assistance Program
All employees have access to confidential counseling and support services through our Employee Assistance Program (EAP). This includes mental health counseling, financial planning assistance, and legal consultation. Services are available 24/7 and are completely confidential.
